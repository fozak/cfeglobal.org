Entrepreneurs as Leaders in Business and Society

August 20, 2016

Annual Conference Focuses on Business Leadership and Society Impact

The Center for Entrepreneurship is holding its annual conference 'Entrepreneurs as Leaders in Business and Society'. The event will provide entrepreneurs with a platform to discuss best practices in leading their company's growth, including how to use information technology, personnel management, raising capital, and the role entrepreneurs play in creating jobs, national wealth, and developing society. The event will be conducted in English with simultaneous interpretation.

Event Agenda

08:30 - 09:00: Registration and welcome coffee

09:00 - 09:40: Welcome Addresses

09:40 - 10:40: Leadership That Lasts: How Entrepreneurs Lead Growth Companies
Dr. Ray Smilor
This master class will focus on the personal dimensions of leadership. It highlights three powerful attributes of leadership — authenticity, trust, and optimism — those that inspire people to follow leaders.

10:40 - 11:00: Coffee Break and Networking

11:00 - 12:30: Information and Internet Technologies for Business Development
Entrepreneurs and experts discuss new trends in the development of information and Internet technologies, how information and mobile technology development will affect future consumer behavior, and using internet and social networking tools to attract and retain customers.

12:30 - 13:45: Lunch

13:45 - 15:15: Break-Out Sessions

  • Session One: Search for talent: attracting and keeping the best personnel
  • Session Two: Raising capital for business growth

15:15 - 15:45: Coffee Break

15:45 - 17:00: Entrepreneurs and Society — Moderated discussion on roles entrepreneurs play in mentoring young entrepreneurs, developing socially responsible businesses, and improving the culture for entrepreneurship.

17:00 - 17:30: Entrepreneur as Leader in Business and Society Keynote

17:30 - 17:45: Closing Remarks

18:00 - 20:00: Entrepreneurship Development Awards and Networking Reception

Event Details

Venue: Radisson Royal Hotel

Conference Times: 9:00 AM – 6:10 PM

Gala Reception: 6:10 PM – 8:00 PM

Participation Fee:

  • For entrepreneurs: 6,000 RUB (including VAT)
  • For participants from other cities: 4,800 RUB (including VAT)

Key Organizations Involved

Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year Program recognizes leaders who drive sustainable growth in their businesses. Their objective is to unite and engage with the entrepreneurs driving successful businesses, celebrating their achievements across various categories.

Another notable organization is Cyprus Investment Promotion Agency (CIPA), which aims to enhance and promote Cyprus as an attractive international investment destination. They provide support and necessary information to potential investors aiming to establish or expand their businesses.

The conference is supported by various strategic partners, sponsors, and information partners, ensuring that entrepreneurs gain valuable insights and networking opportunities.
