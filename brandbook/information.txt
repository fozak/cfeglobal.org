





CFE Timeline (2002–2006)----------------------------------------------------------------------------
2002

October – CFE launched in Moscow.

November – First training module offered: “Making Winning Presentations to Investors.”

2003

February

CFE launches the first Entrepreneurs’ Organization (EO) Chapter in Moscow — the first EO chapter in Europe.

CFE begins partnership with OPORA.

May

First CFE Russia–U.S. Entrepreneur Exchange.

June

Launch of Entrepreneurship Group business breakfast series.

September

CFE launches operations in St. Petersburg.

CFE organizes Russia–US Entrepreneurial Forum with the Ministry of Industry and Science.

November

CFE sponsors and judges the first Russian Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year Awards.

December

CFE elects two Russian board members.

2004

April

CFE launches operations in Nizhny Novgorod.

November

CFE launches the first Entrepreneurs’ Organization Chapter in St. Petersburg.

CFE launches FastTrac Planning Mini-MBA program.

2005

July

CFE sponsors Russian faculty to participate in the Entrepreneurship Colloquium at Harvard University.

2006

March

Over 10,000 people attend events organized or supported by CFE.

October

CFE launches Dynamic Entrepreneurship Classroom, an annual program to train Russian university faculty in teaching entrepreneurship.

CFE launches Entrepreneurship Insights tri-annual e-bulletin.

Participation Milestones

May (2003) – Over 1,000 people attend CFE events.

November (2004) – Over 5,000 people attend CFE events.

December (2006) – Over 14,000 people attend CFE events.
